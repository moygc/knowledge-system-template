## Context Excluded from Transfer

Confirm that the sheet excludes unnecessary:

- project, client, organization, or personal names;
- locations and identifiers;
- deliverables, schedules, budgets, and commercial conditions;
- private measurements, files, and decisions;
- case-specific parameters that do not define general applicability.

## Handoff Check

- Each requirement is meaningful without knowing its origin.
- Every proposed type describes a knowledge function, not a project deliverable.
- Material applicability conditions and uncertainty remain explicit.
- Search terms are sufficient for manual or automated retrieval.
- No unsupported knowledge claim is presented as established fact.
- The receiving executor does not need access to the originating context.
